About Alab Becker

Alan Becker was born in 1989 in Dublin, Ohio, USA. He is an American online animator, YouTuber, and artist. At first, he created pixel animations, but after some time, he started animating stick figures. Then, he came up with the idea of making an animation about a stick figure fighting the animation program itself, which became one of his main and most popular series and is still being continued today.

Important Events

  1. 1989-Born in Dublin, Ohio.
  2. 2001-Started experimenting with pixel art.
  3. 2006-Created Animator vs. Animation.
  4. 2007-Animator vs. Animation II won a People's Choice Webby Award.
  5. 2013-Graduated from Columbus College of Art and Design.
  6. 2014-Released Animator vs. Animation 4.

Contributions

  • Developed a unique style of stick-figure animation.
  • Created the Animator vs. Animation series.
  • Created the Animation vs. Minecraft series.
  • Created educational animation projects such as Animation vs. Math.
  • Built a successful career as an independent online animator.
